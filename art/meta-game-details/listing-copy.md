## Long description (388 characters)

Tap or swipe to move the top donut onto another stack with room. Match three or more donuts of the same color at the top of one stack to clear them and score points.

Plan a few moves ahead. Moves without a match raise the dough meter and bring the next refill closer. Match on consecutive moves to build a combo, and hit score targets to level up as new colors and stacks come into play.
